# Chronos lesson production

This skill routes work; the [lesson creation runbook](../../../docs/content/lesson-creation-runbook.md) owns production policy. Optimize for ages 11–15, initially drafting for a roughly 12–13-year-old reader subject to actual learner observation. Preserve historical rigor, calm learning, canonical editorial control, stable identity and sincere-attempt completion.

## Classify before loading the workflow

- **Publish an already-approved lesson:** read only `docs/content/lesson-publication.md` and execute it. Do not boot research, rediscover platform skills, repeat completed reviews or search changelogs/advisors. Approval to publish and production access must be real; never infer them from draft status.
- **Create next / continue:** locate the repository, read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/content/lesson-creation-runbook.md`, and `docs/content/lesson-production-queue.md` completely; read their required companion guidance and active Linear/PR context. Follow the runbook boot sequence. Continue active work before selecting the first eligible Ready row. Respect explicit pauses; never advance paused lessons through a workflow rollout.
- **Material revision:** identify the canonical lesson and single research note; re-enter the earliest affected stage. Preserve stable IDs where meaning remains equivalent. Repeat affected owner gates when scope, central explanation, evidence, prompts, media job, completion or journey role changes.
- **Narrow correction:** use Stage 18's correction path and proportionate validation, retaining its direct preview-link handoff.
- **Production-system changes:** follow the user's authorized scope without starting a queued lesson. Inspect relevant uncommitted work read-only; preserve pending gates and unrelated safeguards.

Missing canonical runbook/queue is a prerequisite failure; do not reconstruct policy from chat. Ask for a topic only if the queue and context cannot supply one and a curriculum decision is required.

## Execute the operational path

**Brief → research/material decisions → learning prototype → review → final assets/verification → publication.** Use the runbook's operational table for stage mapping. The single lesson research note owns source/claim records, blueprint and decisions; copy shapes from `docs/content/lesson-production/authoring-templates.md`, not a second checklist.

- Discover broadly; distinguish central supporting, qualifying and discovery sources. Closely review exact passages/figures/objects supporting central claims. Give consequential challenges full analysis and peripheral proposals concise deferral reasons. Apply identical evidentiary questions to inherited and alternative accounts; no source quotas or false balance.
- **Stage 3B remains mandatory during rollout.** Lead the user-facing handoff and research note with a one-screen, numbered `yes / change / no` decision card: recommended focus, provisional teaching beats, and possible visual jobs. Link the analytical packet as optional depth; the owner can answer from the message alone. Retain `Researching` and stop before claim selection or lesson build-out until the owner responds. The runbook's possible routine path is inactive and cannot bypass pending gates.
- Every blueprint includes Retrieve, Extend, Revisit, reasoning progression and an occasional transfer plan. Completion records sincere study/attempts, not demonstrated mastery.
- Build a complete unpublished typed lesson in the real Learn shell. Run `lesson:gate` for prototype and `lesson:preview`. Keep final assets, publication migrations, unlocks and hosted changes behind explicit prototype approval. Review against the quality contract once; identify proxy type honestly, resolve blocking findings, and leave product review pending until the owner approves.
- Select media methods by teaching job using Stage 10: licensed evidence originals; reviewed deterministic/native/vector or raster factual visuals; generated reconstruction/atmosphere when appropriate. Preserve provenance, rights, uncertainty, accessibility and reference/data-versus-final fidelity. No mandatory generative restyling.
- After approval, refine the same draft and review only changed or unresolved surfaces. Complete media lifecycle records, implementation/release gates, content/domain checks; let CI run required full checks. Use the publication playbook only after explicit go-live authorization.

The [learner-observation program](../../../docs/content/lesson-production/learner-observation.md) starts early with sampled representative lessons and interaction risks. Adult/AI proxy review is not evidence of age fit. Real sessions remain pending human participation; child testing is not a gate on every lesson.

## Handoff and integrity

Keep queue/Linear state truthful. Record `Complete` in the lesson PR's final commit before merge; merge ends the process, so never open a closeout PR or verify production afterward. Never approve on the owner's behalf, publish a draft, bypass rights or identity controls, expose private learner explanations, or alter another checkout. Gate scripts prove structure and references, not research quality or human approval.

Every lesson-review handoff includes the current direct lesson preview in the final response, including small corrections; follow the runbook's preview-link/access caveats. Link the single research note and image lifecycle when applicable, PR/issue, validation, actual approval state and remaining human decisions. Never call local checks learner validation or unpublished work published.
