# Chronos candidate journey roster

Linear anchor: [ASH-68 — Map and maintain the canonical Chronos journey portfolio](https://linear.app/ashs-workshop/issue/ASH-68/map-and-maintain-the-canonical-chronos-journey-portfolio)

Status: **Draft portfolio for product-owner and curriculum review.** Inclusion here means “worth retaining as a deliberately shaped candidate,” not published, production-ready, or promised for launch.

## Decision summary

Chronos needs a portfolio map before journey production begins. Without one, the Library will be filled reactively, lesson production will remain almost entirely World-Spine-driven, and “journeys” can decay into tag filters or conventional civilization surveys.

This draft maps four learner-facing shelves while preserving the existing domain model:

- **Civilizations & Regions** — long-form continuity and transformation; domain kind `story_arc`.
- **Stories** — bounded movements, networks, eras, and connected historical processes; domain kind `story_arc`.
- **Ideas Across Time** — cross-regional questions and systems; domain kind `idea_trail`.
- **Investigations** — evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, and revision; domain kind `investigation`.

The first recommended reference portfolio is intentionally small:

1. `story.mesopotamia.cities-and-empires` — **Mesopotamia: Cities, Gods, and Empires**
2. `idea.writing.information-and-power` — **Writing Changes the World**
3. `investigation.uruk.first-tablets` — **What Do the First Tablets Actually Tell Us?**
4. `story.egypt.nile-and-kingship` — **Ancient Egypt: The Nile, Kingship, and Afterlives**
5. `idea.government.who-should-rule` — **Who Should Rule?**
6. `investigation.rapa-nui.collapse-and-adaptation` — **Rapa Nui: Collapse, Adaptation, and Myth**

This sequence proves reuse early: the first three paths can build on Farming and Settlements, Uruk, and Early Writing before Chronos acquires a large optional corpus. Rapa Nui remains the mature Investigation reference, but it should not be the first time the Investigation engine and editorial method are tested.

## Portfolio rules

### A journey earns a place when

- it has a compelling question and defensible pedagogical thesis;
- order changes understanding—the entries cannot be freely shuffled without loss;
- it contributes continuity, comparison, synthesis, or historical reasoning that the World Spine alone cannot provide;
- several canonical lessons can be reused with genuinely different “why it matters here” framing;
- journey-only lesson demand is bounded and historically justified;
- its geographic, chronological, and evidentiary boundaries are explicit;
- it ends with synthesis, transformation, legacy, or unresolved consequence rather than merely reaching the last date.

### Reject, merge, or redirect a proposal when

- it is a tag-filtered list, a ruler parade, a nation-by-nation survey, or a chronology with no thesis;
- one existing lesson, Knowledge Card, search result, or Special Collection would teach it better;
- its main purpose is catalog volume, visual variety, or filling an empty Library shelf;
- it treats a civilization as sealed, timeless, internally uniform, or marching toward a modern nation-state;
- it duplicates another journey without a distinct historical question;
- an Investigation depends on a trick, sensational mystery, or single predetermined answer;
- the available evidence cannot support an honest ages 11–14 experience.

## Priority vocabulary

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `R` Reference | One of six journeys recommended to prove the complete model. |
| `1` Foundation | High-value next-wave journey with strong reuse and curricular leverage. |
| `2` Expansion | Valuable after a larger lesson corpus or a named prerequisite exists. |
| `3` Reserve | Retain for research and future portfolio balance; do not queue automatically. |

Priority is not a ranking of historical importance. It describes product sequencing, reusable-content leverage, source readiness, and the distinct learning job the journey performs.

# 4. Investigations

Investigations teach historical reasoning. A candidate may concern an unresolved question, a once-popular myth, conflicting witnesses, an object/site that supports limited inference, or a scholarly interpretation that changed with new evidence. The learner must be able to distinguish observation, source, inference, interpretation, uncertainty, and later tradition; form a provisional account; and revise it.

## Portfolio balance diagnostics

Before promoting any later candidate, review the portfolio rather than the candidate alone:

- regions and communities already over- or underrepresented;
- ancient/medieval/early-modern/modern balance;
- ordinary life versus rulers, battles, and state institutions;
- environment, labor, gender/family, belief, knowledge, and exchange;
- Indigenous, oral, archaeological, material, manuscript, quantitative, and visual evidence;
- journeys that reuse existing lessons versus those creating large new-content islands;
- whether the same famous topic is being offered as a Story, Idea Trail, Investigation, lesson, and card without distinct learning value.

No quota substitutes for judgment. These diagnostics expose concentration and omission; they do not automatically approve a weak journey.

# 6. Relationship to lesson production

Journey planning may identify a missing lesson, but it does not silently insert that lesson into the canonical World Spine or promote it to `Ready`.

For each journey-only lesson gap:

1. create a stable candidate lesson ID and bounded reason for existing;
2. record which approved journeys require it and whether any World Spine node already covers the material;
3. run the lesson-node justification stage in the canonical lesson creation runbook;
4. classify it as shared, journey-only required, journey-only optional, Investigation evidence content, card/collection content, or rejected;
5. add it to the lesson production queue only through an explicit product/curriculum review;
6. preserve the World Spine’s independent canonical order and readiness decisions.

The eventual journey-production queue should select an active journey blueprint, not automatically cause every missing entry to be produced. A journey may remain in research or staged publication while its justified lesson gaps are reviewed and scheduled.

# 8. Curricular grounding

The portfolio applies the Chronos PRD and approved World Spine, plus these external principles:

- [World History for Us All](https://whfua.history.ucla.edu/) organizes specific histories within global chronological patterns and multiple scales rather than treating civilizations as disconnected containers.
- Its [seven key themes](https://whfua.history.ucla.edu/shared/themes.php) provide a useful coverage diagnostic: population; economic networks; power; inequality; identity; science/technology/environment; and spiritual life/moral codes.
- The [C3 Framework](https://www.socialstudies.org/sites/default/files/c3/C3-Framework-for-Social-Studies.pdf) structures inquiry around compelling questions, disciplinary tools, evidence evaluation, and communicated conclusions.
- The [Digital Inquiry Group](https://www.inquirygroup.org/history-lessons) emphasizes sourcing, contextualization, corroboration, close reading, and claims supported by documentary evidence.
- [UNESCO’s General History of Africa education work](https://www.unesco.org/en/general-history-africa) supports broader, locally grounded perspectives on African histories and diasporas rather than inherited colonial framing.
