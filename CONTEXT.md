# Chronos Curriculum

This glossary defines the authored learning objects that organize Chronos. It keeps topic coverage, lesson identity, and learner paths distinct so the curriculum can be comprehensive without forcing unrelated teaching purposes into one reusable object.

## Language

**Journey**:
An authored, ordered batch of related Lessons with its own question or focus, intentional sequence, and ending. A Journey has one primary Journey Category and may reuse Lessons or contain journey-only Lessons, but it is never a filtered list of topics.
_Avoid_: Arc as the generic term, course, playlist, filtered timeline, shelf

**Journey Boundary Test**:
The editorial test determining whether one long Journey remains coherent or must be split, narrowed, redesigned, or omitted. A Journey passes only when one accumulating learner promise and synthesis, historically honest scope, and prose and conceptual accessibility for roughly ages 10-14 can all hold after reasonable repair; chronology and size are review signals, not automatic boundaries.
_Avoid_: Lesson-count limit, automatic period split, series hierarchy

**Journey Category**:
The single primary reason an optional Journey hangs together: Civilizations & Regions, Stories, Ideas & Questions, or Investigations. Journey Categories are flat peers; Lesson length, form, and subject matter do not create category hierarchy.
_Avoid_: Shelf, subcategory, lesson type

**World History**:
Chronos's singular default chronological Journey and main timeline. It is structurally a Journey but stands outside the optional Journey Categories.
_Avoid_: World Spine in learner-facing language, Library category

**Mature Journey Portfolio**:
The versioned set of optional Journeys whose coverage has been audited together with World History and approved as Chronos's complete educational canon for learners around ages 10-14. It may evolve through renewed admission and proportionate re-audit; maturity does not imply encyclopedic exhaustiveness.
_Avoid_: Complete encyclopedia, frozen canon, launch minimum

**Coverage Disposition**:
The product-owner-approved outcome for a credible portfolio gap: a blocking omission that must be fixed before maturity, an intentional omission with rationale, or a worthwhile future expansion that does not block maturity.
_Avoid_: Covered/uncovered toggle, automatic quota failure

**Civilizations & Regions**:
Journeys organized around continuity and transformation within a civilization, cultural world, or geographic region. The category does not imply that its subject was bounded, homogeneous, urban, state-based, or isolated.
_Avoid_: Society as a generic replacement, civilization survey

**Stories**:
Journeys organized around a bounded chain of people, events, movements, exchanges, or consequences. The narrative relationship among developments, rather than a place alone or a recurring cross-context question, holds the Journey together.
_Avoid_: Story Arc, playlist

**Ideas & Questions**:
Journeys organized around a recurring philosophical question, belief, institution, practice, technology, or system examined across historical contexts. Philosophy may organize a Journey here and remains part of Lessons in every category.
_Avoid_: Idea Trail, History of Ideas when it implies one inevitable lineage

**Investigations**:
Journeys organized around an evidence problem through which the learner forms, tests, or revises a historical interpretation. Mystery or uncertainty alone is insufficient without meaningful historical reasoning from evidence.
_Avoid_: Mystery, puzzle collection

**Lesson**:
A bounded learning experience organized around a particular historical claim, question, moment, or explanatory purpose. Lessons may vary substantially in length and form; two Lessons may concern the same person, event, place, or idea when they teach materially different things, and shared subject matter alone does not make them the same Lesson.
_Avoid_: Topic, content node

**Journey Entry**:
A Lesson's authored place and role within one Journey, including why it matters there and any journey-specific transition or framing. A Journey Entry may reference a shared Lesson or a journey-only Lesson.
_Avoid_: Node when referring to a position in a Journey

**Shared Lesson**:
A Lesson reused across Journeys because its central learning purpose and substantive content remain equivalent. Journey-specific framing may change, but reuse is not forced merely because two Journeys discuss the same subject.
_Avoid_: Canonical topic, universal subject lesson

**Journey-only Lesson**:
A Lesson authored for one Journey because its learning purpose, depth, or context does not warrant reuse elsewhere. It remains a first-class Lesson and may later be reused if another Journey genuinely needs the same learning experience.
_Avoid_: Duplicate lesson, lesser lesson

**Recent History**:
Near-present history with a bounded scope and stable factual core, although interpretation may remain contested.
_Avoid_: Living History, current events

**Ongoing History**:
Canonical history of a still-unfolding process that has passed the heightened evidence and review gates and presents only defensible provisional synthesis.
_Avoid_: Breaking news, Current Context

**Current Context**:
A temporary, versioned, dated optional Journey Entry connecting a qualifying unfolding development to an approved Journey's learning purpose. It never contributes to progress, portfolio maturity, or new Knowledge Card unlocks.
_Avoid_: News lesson, Living History
